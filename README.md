Kikwetu MIS TVET Companion

Smart Digital Student Services, Timetable Management, AI Assistance, and Institutional Accountability Platform for TVET Institutions.

Overview

Kikwetu MIS is an AI-powered digital campus ecosystem designed for TVET institutions. It streamlines student services, academic processes, timetabling, institutional communication, workflow management, and accountability through a unified platform. The system aims to reduce paperwork, eliminate physical queues, improve transparency, and provide a seamless digital experience for students, lecturers, administrators, and institutional management.

Vision

To create a modern, intelligent, and accountable digital environment where students can access institutional services, track requests, manage academic activities, and receive support through a single platform while enabling staff and management to deliver services more efficiently.

Core Objectives
Digitize student services and institutional workflows.
Reduce paper-based processes and repetitive office visits.
Provide transparent request tracking and service delivery.
Simplify POE submissions and verification.
Manage deferment and return processes digitally.
Enable intelligent timetable management and conflict detection.
Provide AI-powered assistance through Kiki.
Deliver management insights through dashboards and analytics.
Improve accountability through audit trails and workflow tracking.

Workflow Modules
1. New-Term Reporting

Students report for a new term digitally, upload required evidence, and receive verification updates.

2. POE Management

Supports document uploads, version control, verification, corrections, approvals, and rejection tracking.

3. Deferment & Return

Allows students to apply for deferment and later request reactivation following institutional approval workflows.

4. Complaints & Service Requests

Provides structured workflows for reporting issues, submitting requests, tracking progress, and receiving resolutions.

5. Timetable Management

Creates conflict-free timetables with validation rules covering lecturers, cohorts, rooms, capacities, and institutional constraints.
